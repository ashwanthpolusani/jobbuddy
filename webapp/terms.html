1. What Rolenaut Is

Rolenaut is an autonomous career exploration engine and open job discovery platform. Our automated indexing spiders aggregate publicly accessible technical job requisitions directly from enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and corporate career domains.

Rolenaut is not an employer, staffing agency, or recruiter. We do not post private employment listings, receive or review candidate applications, or participate in hiring negotiations.

Job listings are indexed and categorized by automated scripts and classification algorithms. While we strive to maintain verified, high-signal listings:

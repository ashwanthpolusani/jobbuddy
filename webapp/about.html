About Rolenaut

An open, transparent tech job discovery platform and scraper hub created to help candidates bypass ghost jobs, recruiter spam, and commercial paywalls.

Founded 2026 • Open Source Compatible • Direct ATS Indexing

1. Why Rolenaut Was Built

Searching for early-career tech jobs is notoriously broken. Traditional job boards are flooded with ghost postings, outdated requisitions, third-party recruiters harvesting resumes to build email lists, and "fresher" listings that secretly demand 3+ years of experience.

Rolenaut was initially created for friends, cousins, and fellow engineers across Indian tech hubs (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Gurgaon) who were preparing to switch roles or break into software engineering. We wanted a straightforward, transparent tool that cuts out the noise: index official company career portals directly, verify role eligibility, and give everyone direct application links with zero middlemen.

2. Direct ATS Sourcing: Zero Middlemen

Instead of aggregating from untrusted job boards or social feeds, Rolenaut connects directly to enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) — including Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and SmartRecruiters.

  • Direct Apply Links: Every job card links straight to the employer's official job requisition. You apply directly into their hiring pipeline.
  • Automated Eligibility Verification: Job specifications are parsed to filter out misleading "fresher" roles that require multi-year professional experience.
  • No Expired Jobs: Postings are synchronized daily directly from source corporate careers endpoints.

3. Two Ways to Use Rolenaut

Rolenaut is designed to be universally accessible for both everyday job seekers and power developers:

1. Default Verified Tech Stream (Free & Instant)

No login required. Anyone visiting Rolenaut can immediately search, filter, and apply to 500+ curated early-career roles across Software Engineering, Data/AI, Cloud/DevOps, Security, and Product.

2. Connect Your Own Scraper Database (Power Users)

Rolenaut is 100% compatible with the open-source rolenaut-engine (jobbuddy-scraper) on GitHub. If you fork the scraper and run it against custom companies or custom roles, you can connect your read-only MongoDB Atlas URI. Rolenaut instantly transforms into your private, personalized frontend.

4. Privacy & Candidate Rights

We believe job search data is deeply personal:

  • Zero Spyware & Tracking Pixels: We do not track you across the web, sell your contact details to recruitment agencies, or monetize your personal search habits.
  • Read-Only Database Security: When power users connect a MongoDB Atlas cluster, Rolenaut recommends a dedicated read-only role (webapp_reader) stored securely in Netlify Database.
  • Full GDPR Compliance: You can export all your account data in JSON format or delete your account permanently with 1 click from your profile.

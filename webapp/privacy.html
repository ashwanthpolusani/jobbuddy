Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Compliant with GDPR & Global Privacy Standards

This Privacy Policy outlines how Rolenaut ("we", "us", or "our") manages, protects, and respects your privacy when you use our platform at rolenaut.netlify.app. We believe job discovery should be transparent, accessible, and respectful of personal autonomy.

1. Information We Collect & Architecture

Rolenaut operates on a privacy-first foundation. You do not need to create an account or provide personal data to search or apply for tech jobs.

  • Guest Mode (Default): Anyone can browse all 500+ live direct-to-ATS job listings, filter by track or location, and apply directly on employer portals without signing up or submitting personal information.
  • Optional Candidate Accounts (Netlify Identity): If you choose to sign in to sync your workspace across devices, authentication is powered securely by Netlify Identity (GoTrue). We receive your verified email address and assign an internal identifier. Passwords are encrypted by Netlify edge infrastructure and are never visible to or stored on our application code.
  • Cloud Profile Storage (Netlify Database - PostgreSQL): For registered users, we store user profile preferences in Netlify Database (PostgreSQL): your display name, bookmarked jobs (saved_jobs), onboarding status, and data source preference (inbuilt or custom).
  • Companion Scraper MongoDB Connection (Optional): If you operate the open-source Rolenaut companion scraper and connect your private MongoDB Atlas cluster, your connection string is stored in your personal profile in Netlify Database. We mandate read-only database user credentials (e.g., webapp_reader). Your raw MongoDB URI is strictly kept server-side and is never exposed in client API responses.
  • Browser Storage (localStorage): To eliminate interface flicker and deliver instant filter updates, your browser stores cached job data and session tokens locally in localStorage. This data stays on your local device.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising

Rolenaut displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to sustain free, open job indexing. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this and other websites. These cookies are placed and governed directly by Google under Google's Advertising Privacy Policies.

You can personalize or disable Google AdSense tracking by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds Consumer Choice.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Processors

Rolenaut relies on industry-standard cloud providers:

  • Netlify: Global hosting, edge functions, serverless functions, Netlify Identity authentication, and Netlify PostgreSQL database. See Netlify Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Cloud document storage for the public curated job feed. No personal candidate identifiers are stored in the job listings database.
  • Google Fonts: High-performance web typography served directly from Google CDN.
  • Clearbit Logo API: Fetches verified corporate brand icons matching employer domains (e.g. amazon.com). No user data is sent.

4. External Applications & Direct-to-ATS Links

Every "Apply" link on Rolenaut connects directly to official corporate Applicant Tracking Systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) or company career domains. Rolenaut never collects, stores, parses, or monetizes your resume, cover letter, or application submissions. Your application data remains strictly between you and the employer.

5. Data Retention & Stale Job Lifecycle

Curated requisitions are synchronized and verified daily. Requisitions that are closed or not re-verified by our scrapers are automatically purged from the active database after 30 days. Candidate account profiles and bookmarked jobs persist until modified or permanently deleted by the user.

6. GDPR & Global Candidate Privacy Rights

Whether you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, California (CCPA/CPRA), or elsewhere, Rolenaut respects and guarantees the following rights:

  • Right to Data Portability (GDPR Article 20): You can download a complete, machine-readable JSON export of your profile, preferences, and saved bookmarks at any time via your Candidate Profile or directly from /api/user-profile?export=true.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (GDPR Article 17): You have the absolute right to permanently erase your profile and all associated data. Clicking "Delete Account" in your Candidate Profile executes an immediate database deletion (DELETE FROM user_profiles), purges all stored connection strings, and clears your browser session.
  • Right to Rectification (GDPR Article 16): You can update your display name, data source preference, or custom scraper connection at any time in your profile settings.
  • Zero Selling of Data: Rolenaut does not sell, rent, license, or trade your personal information, search queries, or email address to third-party recruiters, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • Zero Invasive Tracking Pixels: We do not deploy fingerprinting libraries, session replays, or invasive third-party analytics pixels.

7. Security of Stored Data

All communication with Rolenaut is encrypted via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Authentication tokens are validated server-side using cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Private database strings provided for custom scrapers are stored within isolated PostgreSQL tables accessible only through authenticated serverless execution contexts.

8. Children's Privacy

Rolenaut is a career exploration platform intended for students, graduates, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 16 years of age.

9. Contact & Employer Takedown Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your data rights, or represent an employer requesting the removal of your organization's open career postings, please open a ticket on our public GitHub Issue & Takedown Tracker. Verified employer takedown requests are processed within 48 business hours.

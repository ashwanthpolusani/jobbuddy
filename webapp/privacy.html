Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JobBuddy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our website at buddyjob.netlify.app.

1. Information We Collect

JobBuddy is a read-only job aggregation service. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or submit any personal information to use the site.

We collect limited non-personal information automatically:

  • Usage data — pages viewed, filter selections, and general interaction patterns, collected through Netlify's built-in analytics (aggregated, not individually identifiable).
  • Browser storage — we store a cache of job listings in your browser's localStorage to speed up page loads. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

2. Google AdSense

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by JobBuddy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

3. Third-Party Services

JobBuddy uses the following third-party services:

  • Netlify — hosting and serverless functions. Netlify may collect IP addresses and access logs. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting. Job data (titles, companies, links) is stored here. No personal user data is stored.
  • Google Fonts — font loading. Google may log requests; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Clearbit Logo API — company logos are fetched from Clearbit using the company domain. No personal data is sent.

4. External Job Applications

When you click "Apply" or "Browse" on a job listing, you are redirected to the employer's website or an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) such as Greenhouse, Lever, or Workday. JobBuddy has no visibility into, and does not collect, your resume, cover letter, or application data. The privacy of your application is governed entirely by the employer's privacy policy.

5. Cookies

JobBuddy itself does not set cookies. However, Google AdSense may set cookies on your device for advertising purposes. You can control cookie behaviour through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

Job listings are automatically deleted from our database 30 days after they were last scraped. Your browser cache (localStorage) can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

JobBuddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at support@buddyjob.netlify.app.
